# Inviolable Principles

- The specification is the source of truth; code must be reviewable against it.
- Security, privacy, and human validation are explicit responsibilities, not automatic promises.
- Dependencies and CI actions must be pinned or deliberately updated and reviewed.
- Public content must remain semantically consistent across Portuguese and English editions.
- Do not introduce fictional product examples as if they were kit capabilities.

Changes to these principles require an ADR in `.harness/adr/` because they affect the kit contract.
